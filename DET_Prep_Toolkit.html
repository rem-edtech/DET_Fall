🦉 DET Prep Toolkit

Free websites & tools to build your English skills for the Duolingo English Test — organized by the skills the test measures. Everything here is free to use.

⭐ Official DET 📖 Reading 🎧 Listening ✍️ Writing 🗣️ Speaking 📚 Vocabulary & Grammar
How the DET is scored: your Duolingo English Test report gives you four skill subscores — Reading, Writing, Listening, and Speaking — which combine into four more: Literacy (reading + writing), Comprehension (reading + listening), Conversation (listening + speaking), and Production (speaking + writing). Practicing the four skills below strengthens all of them.

📖 Reading

Build speed and comprehension with graded, leveled texts.

Breaking News English

breakingnewsenglish.com

Practice reading skills with real news stories. There are thousands of articles at seven difficulty levels, and each article comes with many built-in exercises and activities (vocabulary, gap-fills, comprehension checks, and more).

Visit site →

News in Levels

newsinlevels.com

The same news story rewritten at three levels (easy, medium, hard), each with a short audio recording. Great for reading the same content again as it gets more advanced.

Visit site →

British Council — Reading

learnenglish.britishcouncil.org

Free graded reading texts (emails, articles, stories) sorted by CEFR level, each with comprehension quizzes and vocabulary support — good for reading closer to DET passage length.

Visit site →

🎧 Listening

Train your ear with real audio, transcripts, and quizzes.

Randall's ESL Cyber Listening Lab

esl-lab.com

Hundreds of short listening clips sorted from easy to difficult, each with comprehension quizzes and full transcripts you can check afterward.

Visit site →

ELLLO (English Listening Lesson Library)

elllo.org

A free library of thousands of real, unscripted conversations with people from around the world, each with a transcript, vocabulary notes, and a quiz — great for training your ear for different accents, just like on the DET.

Visit site →

VOA Learning English

learningenglish.voanews.com

Free news and lessons read in slower, clear American English, with articles and audio at multiple levels — a gentle way to build listening stamina.

Visit site →

TED Talks

ted.com/talks

Thousands of free talks with searchable transcripts and subtitles in many languages. Turn on English subtitles for extra support, then try listening without them.

Visit site →

✍️ Writing

Get instant feedback and practice organizing your ideas.

Write & Improve (Cambridge)

writeandimprove.com

Free tool from Cambridge English: type or paste a piece of writing and get instant, color-coded feedback plus an estimated CEFR level. Great for practicing DET-style writing prompts and seeing your improvement over time.

Visit site →

Hemingway Editor

hemingwayapp.com

Paste your writing into the free web version and it highlights sentences that are hard to read, overused adverbs, and passive voice — a fast way to make your DET writing responses clearer.

Visit site →

British Council — Writing

learnenglish.britishcouncil.org

Free writing practice tasks by level, with model answers and tips for organizing paragraphs — useful for the short opinion/essay-style prompts on the DET.

Visit site →

🗣️ Speaking

Practice pronunciation and fluency out loud.

YouGlish

youglish.com

Type any word or phrase and instantly hear real people say it in YouTube clips. Free and excellent for checking pronunciation and natural stress before your DET speaking tasks.

Visit site →

ELSA Speak

elsaspeak.com

An AI pronunciation coach app with a free version: record yourself and get instant feedback on individual sounds. (Extra features are paid, but the free tier is enough for daily pronunciation practice.)

British Council — Speaking

Free videos of everyday spoken English plus guided speaking practice activities — helpful for building the fluency the DET's speaking tasks reward.
