In-Flight Announcement

DET PRACTICE · SPEAKING SAMPLE

Now say your opinion out loud. Record yourself on Vocaroo (free, no account needed) and paste the link below so I can listen and send you feedback.

  1. Open vocaroo.com in a new tab. It works on a computer or a phone.
  2. Take 30 seconds to plan: look at your cue card and choose 2–3 words to use. Don't write a full script. Just use notes.
  3. Press the red record button (allow the microphone if asked) and start talking. If you make a mistake, don't stop. Just keep going, like on the real test.
  4. Press stop, then Save & Upload. Copy the link it gives you (it looks like voca.ro/… or vocaroo.com/…).
