Connecting Flights

Short sentences are like short flights: they work, but you keep stopping. This week you'll use connecting words (because, although, however, which…) to join your ideas into one smooth trip. You choose your own level. Go at your own pace. There's no grade, and finishing all the steps counts as attending this week.

Gate Announcements

DET PRACTICE · LISTEN AND TYPE

On the DET, you hear a sentence and type exactly what you hear. You can play it up to 3 times. The words people miss are usually the small grammar ones: a / the, -ed and -s endings, and short forms like he's. Listen for them.

Tip: Use grammar to check your typing. If you heard "she walk to", grammar tells you it must be "walked" or "walks". Capital letters and punctuation are not checked here.

In-Flight Announcement

BONUS FLUENCY · SPEAKING

Connectors help in speaking too: they give you a way to keep going. Record a short answer on Vocaroo (free, no account needed) and paste the link below so I can listen and send you feedback.

  1. Open vocaroo.com in a new tab. It works on a computer or a phone.
  2. Take 30 seconds to plan: choose the connectors you will use. Don't write a full script. Just use notes.
  3. Press the red record button (allow the microphone if asked) and start talking. If you get stuck, say a connector (and… because… so…) and add one more idea.
  4. Press stop, then Save & Upload. Copy the link it gives you (it looks like voca.ro/… or vocaroo.com/…).
