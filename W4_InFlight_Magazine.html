Week 4 · Reading Longer Texts with Confidence

In-Flight Magazine

On a long flight, you don't read every word of the magazine. You look at the titles, find the main idea, and look for the parts you need. This week you'll practice reading like that, one Interactive Reading question type at a time, and then try a full timed set. You choose your own level. Go at your own pace. There's no grade, and finishing all the steps counts as attending this week.

STOP 01~2 min

Choose Your Cabin

Pick the level that feels right today. Your choice changes the articles, the questions, and the time limits on this page. Every cabin counts the same for attendance, and you can switch any time.

STOP 02~4 min

Pre-flight Briefing

DET FORMAT · INTERACTIVE READING

On the DET, Interactive Reading gives you one passage and six questions in about 7 minutes. You must answer each question before you can move on. Match each question type to what it asks you to do: tap a name, then tap its description. A tip appears for each correct match.

STOP 03~6 min

Headline Hunt

DET PRACTICE · TITLE THE PASSAGE + IDENTIFY THE IDEA

Read fast. You only have a short time to skim each text, so don't read every word. Look at the first and last sentences, and look for words that repeat. Then choose the best title or main idea. Each wrong answer is a common trap, and the feedback tells you which one.

TIPThe best answer covers the whole text. It is not one detail (too narrow), not a huge topic (too broad), and not something the text never says (not in the text).
STOP 04~5 min

Fill the Gaps

DET PRACTICE · COMPLETE THE SENTENCES

Here's the first paragraph of this week's practice article. Choose the best word for each gap, then press Check. On the DET, you must fill every gap before you can move on.

TIPFill the easy gaps first. Then read the whole sentence around each hard gap. Grammar helps: after to you need a base verb, after instead of an -ing form, and before a noun an adjective.
STOP 05~5 min

Missing Page

DET PRACTICE · COMPLETE THE PASSAGE

A sentence is missing from the article. Choose the one that connects the sentence before the gap to the sentence after it. When you click an option, it appears in the gap so you can read it in place. Then press Confirm.

TIPFollow the clue words from Week 3: this, these, they, them point back to something, and however, so, as a result show the direction of the ideas. Wrong answers are often about the right topic but in the wrong place.
STOP 06~6 min

Seat Finder

DET PRACTICE · HIGHLIGHT THE ANSWER

On the DET, you answer some questions by highlighting the words in the passage. Choose a question, then select the answer in the article: drag across the words, or tap the first word and then the last word. Press Check when you're ready.

TIPHighlight the words that answer the question, usually a phrase or one sentence. Don't highlight a whole paragraph, and don't stop after one word. If you're not sure, highlight something anyway. Close answers still get partial credit.
STOP 07~10 min

Full Flight

DET PRACTICE · FULL INTERACTIVE READING SET

Now put it all together. This is a new article you haven't seen, with all six question types in the same order as the DET. Use what you practiced today.

STOP 08~5 min

Tell a Friend
